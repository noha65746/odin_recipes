Creamy Pesto Shrimp

Description

This creamy pesto shrimp pasta is perfect for weeknight dinners and is one of our family's favorites.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound linguine pasta
  • cup butter
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/3 cup pesto
  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot with lightly salted water and bring to a rolling boil.
  2. Stir in linguine and return to a boil. Cook pasta uncovered, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite.
  3. Meanwhile, melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. Stir in cream and season with pepper; cook, stirring constantly.
  5. Stir Parmesan cheese into cream sauce until thoroughly mixed. Stir in pesto and cook until thickened, 3 to 5 minutes.
  6. Stir in shrimp and cook until they turn pink, about 5 minutes. Serve sauce over hot linguine.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy!
